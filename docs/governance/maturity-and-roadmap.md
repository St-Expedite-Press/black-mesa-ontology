## Current maturity

The artifact is an early, BFO-anchored conceptual schema with local validation.
It has no demonstrated operational deployment, empirical assay validation,
registered interchange crosswalk, security model, retention policy, or reuse
license.

## Future work

Future work includes assay-specific semantics, durable identifiers, integrity
controls, external mappings, empirical validation, operational governance, and
release licensing. These are open work items, not promised capabilities.
