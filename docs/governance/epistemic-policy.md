## Policy

Record evidence path, rule-set version, confidence context, and mapping fidelity
with a conclusion. Keep a pre-symptomatic indication distinct from a symptomatic
suspicion or confirmed identification. Do not convert missing records, absent
alerts, or a negative result into a broader claim than the evidence supports.

This policy is documentation guidance; enforcement is limited to explicit
constraints in `schema/shapes.ttl`.
