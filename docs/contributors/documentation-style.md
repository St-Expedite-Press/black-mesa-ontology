## Style rules

State whether a claim is implemented, modeled, illustrative, experimental, or
unresolved. Link formal claims to Turtle or SHACL source. Label synthetic
examples and state limitations. Do not document internal source material,
credentials, operational detail, or affiliations in public documentation.

Generated files belong under `docs/reference/` and must not be hand-edited.
