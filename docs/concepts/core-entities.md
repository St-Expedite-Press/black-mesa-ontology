## Entities and processes

Farm, field, and zone are sites. A flight, sample collection, assay, and
custody chain are processes. A specimen, container, and host population are
material entities. A spectral anomaly, assay result, detection, alert, and
imagery reference are information artifacts or records. This separation keeps
a data-derived anomaly from becoming a crop condition and keeps a detection
from becoming an unqualified property of a place.

The authoritative names, descriptions, parents, and property domains are in
the [generated schema reference](../reference/schema-reference.md).
