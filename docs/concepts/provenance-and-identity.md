## Custody

The custody chain is modeled as the ordered whole of acts producing a
detection: flight, anomaly registration, collection, transfer, and assay. A
detection must link to a custody chain in the checked-in SHACL shapes.

This is a conceptual provenance requirement. It does not provide durable IDs,
authentication, authorization, tamper evidence, device attestation, or a
method for reconciling duplicate records. Those remain design work.
