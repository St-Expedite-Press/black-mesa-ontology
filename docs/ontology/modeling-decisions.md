## Decisions

Detection is an assertion, alerting is separate from detection, anomalies are
data-derived records rather than crop conditions, and raw imagery is referenced
rather than tiled into RDF. Confidence tiers are ordinal terms rather than
probabilities, and pre-symptomatic indication remains a hypothesis that
licenses sampling.

These choices do not settle device identity, assay semantics, crosswalk content,
security, or regulatory reporting.
