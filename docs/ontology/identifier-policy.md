## Schema identifiers

Local schema terms use the `https://example.org/bmo/` namespace. This is a
project-local placeholder namespace, not an assertion of a registered public
identifier service. External taxa are expected to be referenced by external
identifiers.

Record-level URI minting, persistence, collision handling, identity resolution,
and retention policy are not specified. A URI and checksum do not establish
storage availability or identity by themselves.
