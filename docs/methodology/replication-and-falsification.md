## Falsifiable claims

The only claims this repository can directly test are structural: a graph can
conform to documented constraints or fail them. Claims about early detection
require independently specified datasets, ground truth, assay validation,
protocol versions, and predeclared error measures.

Replication should preserve identifiers and provenance sufficient to inspect
the sensing, sampling, and assay path. A successful parse or SHACL result is
not empirical replication.
