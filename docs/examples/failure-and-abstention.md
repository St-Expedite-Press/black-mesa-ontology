## Abstention

Source: authored synthetic example. Evidence type: deliberate invalid fixture.
Verification status: structural only. Authority: SHACL test suite. Limitation:
rejection of a graph does not establish biological truth.

An imagery reference without a checksum, a detection without custody, or a
confidence assessment without assay applicability fails the relevant shape. An
anomaly without confirmation may remain a pre-symptomatic indication or lead
to no detection. Non-detection, missing data, and a failed validation are not
evidence of safety, absence, intent, or certainty.
