## Audit result

The canonical source is `projects/black-mesa-ontology` in this monorepo. A
public repository is generated by `tools/publish_repo.py --project
black-mesa-ontology`; it is not a durable editing target. The authored
normative artifacts are `schema/bmo-core.ttl` and `schema/shapes.ttl`.

## Evidence classification

The source tree contains internal converted project records. They describe
intent, scope, and desired future integrations; they are not public evidence
of implementation or scientific performance and are excluded from assembly.
The checked-in fixtures are synthetic. The schema has no authored NPDN/CAP
mapping entries, no empirical dataset, and no committed license.

## Terminology and claim audit

The schema treats a detection as an assertion, an anomaly as a derived datum,
and an alert as separate. Previous documentation could be read as stronger
than implementation around crosswalk content, full ontology status, and
operational capability. Generated references are authoritative for local term
inventories and prevent comments from silently drifting.
