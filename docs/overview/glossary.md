# Glossary

**Assertion** — an information object making a claim that can have provenance, evidence, versioning, and review.

**Sensor observation** — an observation event performed by a sensing device or procedure. Not the same as its interpretation.

**Observed anomaly** — a derived record that an observation differs from an expected/reference condition. **Not the same as a diagnosis.**

**Sensor indication** — an assertion that sensor evidence warrants investigation. **Not the same as pathogen detection.**

**Diagnostic hypothesis** — a revisable candidate explanation for an anomaly.

**Candidate set** — the live collection of competing diagnostic hypotheses.

**Sampling recommendation** — a directive describing material/evidence to collect next; the ontology does not implement the optimization algorithm.

**Specimen** — physical material collected from a target and given persistent identity.

**Aliquot** — a physically separated portion of a specimen with its own identity and custody history.

**Custody event** — a recorded collection, transfer, receipt, storage, aliquoting, or related process affecting specimen possession or integrity.

**Custody chain** — the real sequence of custody processes.

**Custody record** — information describing custody events. **Not the same as the physical specimen or process.**

**Diagnostic procedure** — an analysis process applied to a specimen or aliquot.

**Diagnostic result** — information produced by a diagnostic procedure.

**Diagnostic disposition** — Confirmed, Suspected, Not Detected, or Undetermined. **Not the same as procedure result or confidence.**

**Evidence stage** — where evidence sits in the investigation.

**Confidence assessment** — a multi-axis description of evidentiary weight.

**Calibrated probability** — a probability emitted by a genuinely calibrated probabilistic model. **Not the same as an arbitrary score or rank.**

**Spatial support** — the area/point/plant/row/footprint over which information is warranted.

**Diagnostic detection assertion** — a scientific assertion derived from diagnostic evidence and linked to custody, disposition, stage, confidence, version, and time.

**Regulatory determination** — a rule/authority-dependent policy assertion. **Not the same as scientific detection.**

**Reporting decision** — a decision about whether/how a determination is transmitted externally.

**Alert** — a directive or notification in the policy layer.

**Reporting rule** — a versioned record describing a jurisdictional policy rule as understood by the project.

**Crosswalk** — an explicit mapping to an external scheme with declared fidelity, including no defensible mapping.

**Pilot pathosystem** — reference-data profile selecting one host, one pathogen, and a scenario role.

**Operational jurisdiction** — deployment/reporting jurisdiction represented as instance data.
